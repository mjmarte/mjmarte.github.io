Postdoctoral Research Fellow

Manuel Jose Marte

Johns Hopkins University

Computational methods for poststroke language recovery.

Language disorders are among the most common and consequential sequelae of stroke, yet the standard tools used to assess and predict recovery remain limited. My research applies natural language processing, large language models, machine learning, and neuroimaging to study poststroke language across the trajectory of recovery.

I completed my Ph.D. in Speech, Language & Hearing Sciences with Swathi Kiran at Boston University and Einat Liebenthal at McLean Hospital, Harvard Medical School. Previously I practiced as a speech-language pathologist in stroke and TBI neurorehabilitation.
