SCORE Lab · Dept. of Neurology · T32 NRSA Fellow at Kennedy Krieger

Language disorders are among the most common and consequential sequelae of stroke, yet the standard tools used to assess and predict recovery remain limited. My research applies NLP, large language models, machine learning, and neuroimaging to study poststroke language function across the trajectory of recovery.
