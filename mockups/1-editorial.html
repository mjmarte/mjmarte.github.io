About

Language disorders are among the most common and consequential sequelae of stroke, yet the standard tools used to assess and predict recovery remain limited. My research applies natural language processing, large language models, machine learning, and neuroimaging to study poststroke language function across the trajectory of recovery.

I completed my Ph.D. in Speech, Language & Hearing Sciences with Dr. Swathi Kiran (Center for Brain Recovery, Boston University) and Dr. Einat Liebenthal (McLean Hospital, Harvard Medical School). Previously I practiced as a speech-language pathologist in TBI and stroke neurorehabilitation.
